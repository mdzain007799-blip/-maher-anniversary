Chapter one

It started with a hello...

Maher, mujhe honestly humari friendship ki har chhoti detail yaad nahi, but ek baat pakki hai — I'm genuinely glad ki hum mile.

Shuru mein tu bas ek online person thi jisse main baat karta tha. Mujhe zara bhi idea nahi tha ki random conversations, stupid jokes aur reels ke beech tu meri life ke favourite logon mein se ek ban jayegi.

Funny hai na? Hum ek dusre ke paas physically nahi hain, na saath baithna hota hai, na randomly milne chale jaate hain. Phir bhi tu meri daily life ka itna important part ban gayi. Bas chats, games, reels aur woh apni faltu bakchodi — aur pata hi nahi chala kab tu itni close ho gayi.

Ab almost ek saal hone wala hai. Exact date humein yaad nahi, October ya November ke aas-paas hi shuru hua tha, but date se zyada important woh saare din hain jo humne is friendship mein spend kiye.

I'm really glad internet ne tujhe meri life mein bheja. Warna meri daily life ka sabse entertaining headache mujhe kahan milta? ♡

but it doesn't get to decide who matters.”

What I want you to remember

Tu har din funny rehne ke liye nahi bani. Tujhe har waqt sabko khush karna, sabke liye kuch extraordinary karna, ya hamesha strong dikhna zaroori nahi hai. Kabhi tired ho, kabhi quiet ho, kabhi khud ko useless feel kare — tu mere saamne bas tu reh sakti hai.

Tu kabhi kabhi sochti hai ki tu dusron ke liye enough nahi karti, ya tu kuch khaas efforts nahi kar rahi. But Maher, tujhe pata bhi hai tune mere liye kitni meaningful cheezein ki hain? Kal jo tune mere liye itna pyaara letter-picture banaya tha na, it genuinely meant a lot to me. Main shayad har baar properly express nahi karta, but I noticed. I cared. I still do.

Effort hamesha grand gestures nahi hota. Kabhi kisi ko yaad rakhna, uski baat sunna, uske saath apna time share karna, ya bas uski life mein consistently present rehna bhi bohot hota hai. Aur tu mere liye woh sab karti hai — apne tareeke se.

Chapter three

Our very serious nonsense ♡

Humari friendship ka official record banaya jaaye toh aadha pyaar hoga aur aadha ek dusre ka dimaag kharab karne ka evidence.

Daily arguments, obviously

Hum din mein pata nahi kitni baar lad lete hain. Kabhi chhoti si baat pe, kabhi meri bakwaas pe, kabhi bas because hum dono ko chain se rehna pasand hi nahi. Phir thodi der baad aise normal ho jaate hain jaise kuch hua hi nahi.

Zain chooses violence

Main tujhe jaan-boojh ke ragebait karta hoon, phir innocent banne ki acting karta hoon. Tu gussa hoti hai aur main andar hi andar sochta hoon, “haan, mission successful.” Main maanta hoon, main kabhi kabhi hadd se zyada annoying hoon. 😭

Zaiba enters the conversation

Zaiba ka naam aate hi mera drama shuru — muh phula lena, fake gussa karna, ignore karne ka natak karna. Jaise mujhe bohot jealousy ho rahi ho. Madam, main bas acting karta hoon… mostly. Tujhe chidhana bhi toh meri unpaid full-time job hai. 💀

The world's most predictable boob jokes

Aur haan, tere boobs pe joke nikaalne ki meri talent ko bhi award milna chahiye. Koi bhi topic ho, main somehow usse wahin le aata hoon. 😭 I know main bohot cringe aur irritating ho jaata hoon, aur agar kabhi joke sach mein hurt kare toh mujhe bol dena — mazaak tabhi mazaak hai jab hum dono hans rahe hon.

The legendary two-minute reset

Hum ladte hain, gussa hote hain, dramatic ban jaate hain… aur phir literally thodi der baad reels bhejna shuru. Humari friendship ke breakup ka duration shayad duniya mein sabse kam hai.

50 reels = 50 replies

Normal log reel pe react karke aage badh jaate hain. Hum? Agar 50 reels bheji hain toh 50 ke 50 ka reply chahiye. Ek reel ka jawab pending ho toh case khul sakta hai. Reel spam is not a hobby; it's our communication system.

Minecraft AI reels and random games

Woh Minecraft AI wali reels dekh-dekh ke apne maze, games khelna, random baatein karna — ye sab chhoti cheezein hain, but meri routine ka favourite part ban gayi hain. Hum kitni bhi bakwaas kar lein, tere saath time pass kabhi boring nahi lagta.

No day without you

Honestly, aisa din yaad nahi aata jisme humne bilkul baat hi na ki ho. Kabhi lambi baatein, kabhi sirf reels, kabhi game, kabhi ek dusre ki jaan kha lena — but somehow tu din ka part hoti hi hai.

“You are my favourite headache.”

And no, this is not an invitation to become even more annoying. Teri lifetime membership already approved hai. ♡

A letter, just for you

Dear Maher, ♡

Maher,

Ye letter likhte hue bhi mujhe samajh nahi aa raha ki shuru kahan se karun, because tu mere liye jitni important hai na, usko normal words mein explain karna thoda mushkil hai. Main toh usually tujhe tang karta rehta hoon, ragebait karta hoon, faltu jokes maarta hoon aur phir jab tu gussa hoti hai toh aise behave karta hoon jaise maine kuch kiya hi nahi. But in sab bakwaas ke peeche ek genuine baat hai: I'm really, really glad ki tu meri life mein hai.

Ek saal hone wala hai humari friendship ko. Hum online mile the, aur shayad kisi aur ko humari chats dekh ke lage ki bas do pagal log ek dusre ka dimaag kha rahe hain. Aur honestly, woh galat bhi nahi honge. 😭 Hum ladte hain, ek dusre ko chidhate hain, main Zaiba ke naam pe fake jealous hota hoon, tere boobs pe har doosri baat ko joke bana deta hoon, aur phir kuch hi der baad hum reels spam kar rahe hote hain jaise duniya mein sabse normal cheez wahi ho.

But mere liye ye sab sirf time pass nahi hai. Ye meri daily life ka woh part hai jiska main aadat se bhi zyada ho chuka hoon. Tera message, teri bakwaas, tera reaction, humare games, Minecraft AI wali reels pe hasna — ye sab mere din ko apna sa bana dete hain. Tu online friend hai, haan. Lekin online hone se koi connection fake nahi ho jaata. Main tujhe saamne baith ke shayad roz nahi dekh sakta, but that doesn't make our friendship any less real to me.

Ek baat hai jo main chahta hoon tu sach mein yaad rakhe. Tu kabhi kabhi sochti hai ki tu dusron ke liye enough nahi karti, ki tu efforts nahi kar paati, ya tu kisi ke liye kuch khaas nahi kar rahi. Kaash tu ek baar meri nazron se khud ko dekh paati. Tujhe shayad apni chhoti chhoti cheezein normal lagti hain, but mere liye woh matter karti hain. Kal tune mere liye jo pyaara sa letter-picture banaya tha, usne mujhe genuinely khush kar diya. It meant a lot, Maher. Sirf isliye nahi ki tune kuch banaya, balki isliye ki tune mere baare mein socha, time diya, aur apne tareeke se mujhe feel karaya ki main matter karta hoon.

Tujhe har baar koi huge effort karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Tujhe mujhe prove karne ki zaroorat nahi hai ki tu achhi friend hai. Tu already hai. Teri presence hi mere liye ek safe, peaceful si jagah jaisi hai — like a little haven. Tere saath main stupid ho sakta hoon, apni bakwaas kar sakta hoon, random cheezon pe has sakta hoon, aur bas apna normal self reh sakta hoon. Har din perfect nahi hota, aur hum dono bhi perfect nahi hain, but I love that hum apni nonsense ke saath bhi ek dusre ki life ka part hain.

Main ye nahi bolunga ki hum kabhi nahi ladenge, because let's be real, hum toh shayad kal bhi ladenge. 😭 Main tujhe phir ragebait karunga, tu mujhe phir daantegi, main fake innocent banunga, aur phir koi reel aa jayegi aur case close. But I hope jab kabhi life genuinely difficult ho, jab tu khud pe doubt kare, ya jab tujhe lage ki tu kisi ke liye enough nahi hai, toh tu ye letter yaad kare. Tujhe apni worth meri ya kisi aur ki approval se prove nahi karni. Tu matter karti hai — even on the days when you don't feel like you do.

Thank you for being here. Thank you for the random chats, the laughs, the games, the reels, the arguments that never last, and all those little moments jo quietly meri life ka important part ban gaye. Thank you for being Maher — apni wali Maher, jise main tang bhi karta hoon aur jiske saath baat kiye bina din ajeeb sa lagta hai.

I don't know exactly kis date ko humari friendship start hui thi. October tha ya November, exact date missing hai. But maybe that's okay. Humari friendship kisi calendar date se important nahi bani; woh important bani because we kept showing up for each other, day after day, reel after reel, argument ke baad argument ke baad bhi.

Happy almost one year to us, meri favourite person. I hope aage bhi humare paas bohot saari stupid memories hon, bohot saare games, bohot saari reels, bohot saari hasi — aur haan, thodi kam fighting… okay, ye promise main bhi nahi kar sakta. 💀

Bas itna yaad rakh: tu mere liye “kuch nahi kar paane wali” person nahi hai. Tu woh person hai jiski presence se ordinary din bhi better lag sakta hai. And that is not a small thing. Not even a little.

I'm really glad it's you, Maher. Out of all the people I could've met online, I'm glad I got to know you.

Always your certified headache,
Zain ♡

Chapter five

A little promise

Promise ye nahi ki hum kabhi nahi ladenge — because hum dono ko jaante hain. 😭

Promise bas itna hai ki main chahta hoon humari friendship aisi jagah bani rahe jahan hum dono apne real selves ho sakein. Jahan mazaak ho, pagalpan ho, honesty ho, aur jab kisi baat se sach mein hurt ho toh hum bol sakein. Jahan kisi ko har waqt perfect ya entertaining banne ki zaroorat na ho.

Maher, jab tu khud pe doubt kare, toh please apne aap ko sirf un cheezon se judge mat karna jo tu kar nahi paayi. Un logon ko bhi yaad karna jinke din mein teri presence farq laati hai. Mere liye, tu un logon mein se ek hai.

Here's to our first year, and to all the chapters we haven't written yet. More laughs, more games, more reels, more nonsense — and hopefully a lifetime supply of things to annoy each other about.
